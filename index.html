How the scores work

Bet–Odds Score = percentage of moneyline bets × decimal moneyline odds. Money–Odds Score = percentage of moneyline handle × decimal moneyline odds. Gap = money % minus bets %. A positive gap suggests larger average wagers on that side relative to its opponent, but does not establish professional action or a profitable bet.

These scores measure market interest adjusted for price, not predicted win probability or expected return. Long-shot teams can score highly even if they are poor bets. Game status is estimated from scheduled start time, not a live scoreboard. Data may be delayed and differs by jurisdiction. Missing data is shown as unavailable, never estimated.
